---
description: Model a priced round on today's fully diluted cap table: price per share, SAFE and note conversions, a pool top-up, and everyone's stake before and after.
---

Ask for the raise and the pre-money valuation, and whether the investors want a post-money pool (for example 10%). Run `npm run cap -- round-model --company=<code> --raise=<amount> --pre-money=<amount> [--pool=10] [--investor="<name>"]`. Show the price, post-money, the dilution table, and one line on what the pool top-up costs the founders. Say the method: conversions and top-up come out of the pre-money. Nothing is written.
